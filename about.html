Leading the Blue Revolution.

Dr.Fishco Solutions Private Limited formulates and markets customised fish feed that helps Indian farmers grow more fish, at lower cost, in cleaner water.

Who we are

A feed company built around the farmer.

India's aquaculture is growing fast, and feed is the largest cost on any fish farm. We started Dr.Fishco with a simple belief: when feed is formulated for the actual species, region and water a farmer works with, everything else improves. Growth is faster, FCR is lower and ponds stay healthy.

That is why every Ulti feed is customised. We control ingredient ranges tightly, keep aflatoxin below 0.01 ppm, and back every bag with advice from our aqua technicians.

Our mission

To make every pond in India more productive and more sustainable, one bag at a time.

What drives us

Our principles.

Precision formulation

Species-specific, geography-aware recipes with defined ingredient ranges and nutrient minimums on every grade.

Uncompromised quality

Aflatoxin B1 below 0.01 ppm and full nutritional disclosure printed on every bag we sell.

Clean water, always

Feeds designed for zero water pollution, because healthy ponds are the foundation of a profitable farm.

Farmer first

Our aqua technicians guide feeding practice on the ground, not just at the point of sale.

Regional focus

Rooted in Andhra Pradesh, India's aquaculture heartland, and growing into other states.

Reliable supply

Consistent 40 kg packs and a growing dealer network so farmers get feed when they need it.
